ACERDEN Projects & Innovations

For over 25 years, ACERDEN has implemented transformative development projects across Nigeria. From sustainable agriculture and climate resilience to health, education, and women & youth empowerment, our initiatives deliver lasting impact and innovative solutions.

ACERDEN's projects span multiple sectors and are designed to empower communities, strengthen institutions, and deliver measurable results.

Past Projects Spotlight

ACERDEN's legacy is built on decades of impactful projects across Nigeria. Here are some highlights:

HIV/AIDS Awareness & Peer Educator Monitoring (2002–2010)

2002 - 2010

Capacity building, advocacy, and health interventions in rural communities to combat HIV/AIDS through peer education and community engagement.

Millennium Development Goals (MDGs) Monitoring Reports (2003–2015)

2003 - 2015

Documenting progress and informing policy decisions through comprehensive monitoring and reporting on MDG achievements across Nigeria.

Technical Vocational & Entrepreneurial Skills Acquisition Workshops (2008)

2008

Empowering girls and youth with marketable skills through hands-on training in various trades and entrepreneurship.

Women Empowerment Strategic Planning Workshops (2002–2003)

2002 - 2003

Promoting gender equity and leadership opportunities for women through strategic planning and capacity building workshops.

Environmental Strategy & Policy Workshops (2003–2010)

2003 - 2010

Building sustainable community-based environmental programs through strategy development and policy workshops.

ACERDEN Innovations

Innovation is at the heart of ACERDEN's work. We develop new approaches to tackle Nigeria's development challenges.

Community-Led Monitoring & Evaluation Tools

Strengthening accountability and transparency through participatory monitoring systems that involve community members in tracking project outcomes.

Climate-Smart Farming Techniques

Reducing post-harvest losses and improving yields through innovative, climate-resilient agricultural practices tailored to local conditions.

Digital Knowledge Platforms

Creating accessible online platforms for research dissemination, policy briefs, and community learning to bridge information gaps.

Integrated Women & Youth Empowerment Model

Combining skills training, leadership development, and entrepreneurship support in a holistic approach to empowerment.

Nutrition & Health Interventions

Linking food security with education and health outcomes through integrated community-based nutrition programs.

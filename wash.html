Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH) Nigeria

ACERDEN improves access to safe water, adequate sanitation, and hygiene education to protect health, dignity, and livelihoods in rural and peri-urban communities across Nigeria.

Support our work

Program Overview

Access to clean water, proper sanitation, and hygiene education remains a critical challenge for many Nigerian communities. Poor WASH conditions contribute to waterborne diseases, malnutrition, school absenteeism, and reduced economic productivity.

ACERDEN’s Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH) programs adopt an integrated, community-driven approach to improve water access, promote safe sanitation practices, and strengthen hygiene awareness. Our interventions prioritize sustainability, community ownership, and public health impact.

Key Focus Areas

Safe Water Access:

Construction and rehabilitation of boreholes, wells, and community water systems.

Sanitation Infrastructure:

Support for household and community latrines and sanitation facilities.

Hygiene Promotion & Behaviour Change:

Community and school-based hygiene education campaigns.

WASH in Schools & Health Facilities:

Improving water and sanitation access in learning and healthcare environments.

Community Management & Sustainability:

Training local water committees for long-term maintenance and ownership.

Program Implementation Approach

Community Needs Assessment:

Participatory identification of WASH gaps and priorities.

Infrastructure Development:

Delivery of durable, context-appropriate water and sanitation solutions.

Capacity Building:

Training community WASH committees, volunteers, and local artisans.

Behaviour Change Communication:

Hygiene promotion using culturally appropriate tools.

Monitoring & Evaluation:

Regular assessment of water quality, facility use, and health outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions on ACERDEN WASH Programs

Which communities benefit from ACERDEN’s WASH programs?

Rural and peri-urban communities with limited access to safe water and sanitation are prioritized.

Does ACERDEN only build boreholes?

No. ACERDEN also rehabilitates existing water systems, supports sanitation infrastructure, and delivers hygiene education.

How does ACERDEN ensure the sustainability of WASH facilities?

Through community ownership models, training of water committees, and local maintenance plans.

Are schools and health facilities included in WASH programs?

Yes. ACERDEN prioritizes schools and health centres to improve health outcomes and learning environments.

How is hygiene behaviour change promoted?

Through community sensitization, school programs, demonstrations, and culturally appropriate communication materials.

Can partners support specific WASH projects?

Yes. Partners can support infrastructure development, hygiene campaigns, research, or capacity-building initiatives.

How is the impact of WASH programs measured?

Through water quality testing, facility usage monitoring, health indicators, and community feedback.

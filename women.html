Women & Youth Development

ACERDEN advances gender equality and youth empowerment by equipping women and young people with the skills, resources, and leadership opportunities needed to drive sustainable social and economic development.

Partner With ACERDEN

Program Overview

Women and youth represent the majority of Nigeria’s population, yet they are disproportionately affected by poverty, unemployment, limited access to education, and social exclusion.

ACERDEN’s Women & Youth Development programs address these challenges by strengthening skills, promoting leadership, expanding livelihood opportunities, and fostering meaningful participation in community development and governance processes.

Key Focus Areas

Economic Empowerment:

Skills training, entrepreneurship support, and income-generation initiatives.

Youth Employability & Skills Development:

Vocational training, digital skills, and life-skills education

Women’s Leadership & Participation:

Supporting women’s inclusion in decision-making structures.

Gender Equality & Social Inclusion:

Addressing barriers to participation and access to resources.

Youth Engagement & Civic Participation:

Encouraging youth leadership in community and development initiatives.

Mentorship & Capacity Building:

Coaching and mentorship for women and youth leaders.

Program Implementation Approach

Needs-Driven Design:

Programs shaped by local realities and participant needs.

Community Partnerships:

Working with women’s groups, youth associations, and local institutions.

Skills-Based Training:

Practical, market-relevant skill development.

Integrated Service Delivery:

Linking health, nutrition, education, and social services.

Monitoring & Evaluation:

Tracking economic, social, and leadership outcomes.

Key Achievements

Increased income-generating opportunities for women and youth
Enhanced participation of women in community leadership roles
Improved employability and life skills among young people
Strengthened local women and youth organizations

Frequently Asked Questions on Women & Youth Development Programs

Who can participate in women and youth programs?

Women and young people in rural and peri-urban communities, particularly those facing economic or social barriers.

What types of skills are offered?

Vocational, entrepreneurial, digital, leadership, and life skills based on community needs.

Are young people without formal education eligible?

Yes. Programs are inclusive and adapted to varying education levels.

Does ACERDEN support access to finance?

ACERDEN facilitates linkages to savings groups, cooperatives, and financial partners where possible.

How does ACERDEN promote gender equality?

By supporting women’s leadership, addressing participation barriers, and engaging communities on inclusion.

Are youth involved in community decision-making?

Yes. Youth participation is encouraged in leadership and civic engagement initiatives.

Can partners co-design women and youth programs?

Yes. ACERDEN welcomes collaborative program design with partners and donors.

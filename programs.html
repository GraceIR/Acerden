Agriculture

Agriculture, Nutrition & Food Security Nigeria

ACERDEN strengthens rural livelihoods by promoting sustainable agriculture, improving household nutrition, and enhancing food security across Nigerian communities.

Program Overview

Agriculture, Nutrition & Food Security is at the core of ACERDEN’s mission to ensure that every community has reliable access to sufficient, nutritious food. Our programs empower smallholder farmers with climate-smart agricultural techniques, value chain support, and household-level nutrition education. We integrate research, capacity building, and community participation to deliver long-term food security and improved nutrition outcomes.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Climate-Smart Agriculture: Training farmers in resilient farming methods to adapt to changing climate conditions.
  • Smallholder Support & Value Chains: Linking farmers to markets, cooperatives, and post-harvest solutions.
  • Nutrition & Food Security: Promoting balanced diets, nutrition awareness, and food storage best practices.
  • Capacity Building & Training: Equipping local farmers with skills in sustainable farming, agribusiness, and nutrition practices.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Community-Led Planning: Needs assessments conducted in partnership with local farmers.
  • Participatory Training: Hands-on workshops on crop management, livestock care, and nutrition education.
  • Research & Innovation: Use of applied research to inform program design and scaling strategies.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation: Impact measured through baseline/endline studies and continuous program monitoring.

Key Achievements

5,000+ farmers trained in climate-smart agricultural practices
10,000+ households reached with nutrition education programs
15 agricultural cooperatives supported and linked to local markets
Poverty Reduction

Poverty Reduction Programs | ACERDEN Nigeria

ACERDEN implements strategic programs to alleviate poverty and create economic opportunities for rural and peri-urban communities in Nigeria. We focus on empowering households, promoting sustainable livelihoods, and fostering long-term community resilience.

Program Overview

Poverty remains a major barrier to sustainable development in many Nigerian communities. ACERDEN’s Poverty Reduction programs aim to tackle systemic economic challenges by promoting income-generating opportunities, financial literacy, social safety nets, and inclusive community development. By combining research, community participation, and integrated interventions, our programs create lasting change and improved livelihoods.

Key Focus Areas

  • Income Generation & Livelihoods: Supporting small businesses, cooperatives, and micro-enterprises.
  • Financial Inclusion & Literacy: Promoting savings, loans, and budgeting skills for households.
  • Skills Development & Vocational Training: Equipping youth and women with marketable skills.
  • Community-Based Initiatives: Strengthening local institutions, cooperatives, and social networks.
  • Social Safety & Empowerment: Facilitating access to government or NGO support programs for the most vulnerable households.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Community-Led Planning: Needs assessments and participatory program design with local stakeholders.
  • Capacity Building:Training on entrepreneurship, financial literacy, and livelihood strategies.
  • Research & Innovation:Applied research informs effective interventions and scaling strategies.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation:Impact tracked via household income, employment metrics, and program feedback.

Why Poverty Reduction Programs Work

  • Evidence-Based: Programs informed by rigorous research and local data.
  • Community-Centered:Designed with active input from beneficiaries.
  • Integrated Approach: Combines skills, entrepreneurship, social protection, and community cohesion.
  • Sustainable: Focused on long-term economic empowerment and measurable results.

Key Achievements

15,000+ households supported with income-generating activities
8,000+ individuals trained in vocational and entrepreneurial skills
50+ community cooperatives strengthened
Water & Hygiene

Clean Water Access Project

ACERDEN implements community-led WASH programs in Nigeria to improve access to safe water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene education in rural and underserved communities.

Program Overview

Access to clean water, proper sanitation, and hygiene education remains a critical challenge for many Nigerian communities. Poor WASH conditions contribute to waterborne diseases, malnutrition, school absenteeism, and reduced economic productivity. ACERDEN’s Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH) programs adopt an integrated, community-driven approach to improve water access, promote safe sanitation practices, and strengthen hygiene awareness. Our interventions prioritize sustainability, community ownership, and public health impact.

Key Focus Areas

  • Safe Water Access: Construction and rehabilitation of boreholes, wells, and community water systems.
  • Sanitation Infrastructure: Support for household and community latrines and sanitation facilities.
  • Promotion & Behaviour Change: Community and school-based hygiene education campaigns.
  • WASH in Schools & Health Facilities: Improving water and sanitation access in learning and healthcare environments.
  • Community Management & Sustainability: Training local water committees for long-term maintenance and ownership.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Community Needs Assessment: Participatory identification of WASH gaps and priorities.
  • Infrastructure Development: Delivery of durable, context-appropriate water and sanitation solutions.
  • Capacity Building: Training community WASH committees, volunteers, and local artisans.
  • Behaviour Change Communication: Hygiene promotion using culturally appropriate tools.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation: Regular assessment of water quality, facility use, and health outcomes.

Featured Project Highlights

  • Community Borehole Rehabilitation Project:Providing reliable water access to rural households.
  • School WASH Initiative:Improving sanitation and hygiene education for students.
  • Hygiene Promotion Campaigns:Training community WASH committees, volunteers, and local artisans.
  • Behaviour Change Communication:Reducing diarrheal diseases through behaviour change.

Why WASH Programs Work

  • Preventive Health Impact:Reduces waterborne and hygiene-related diseases.
  • Community Ownership: Local committees manage and sustain facilities.
  • Integrated Approach: Links water, sanitation, hygiene, health, and education.
  • Sustainability-Focused:Emphasizes long-term functionality and maintenance.

Key Achievements

200+ communities reached with hygiene education
68% Significant reduction in reported waterborne diseases in intervention areas
40% Improved school attendance linked to better WASH facilities
Environment

Environment Conservation & Climate Resilience

ACERDEN implements community-led environmental conservation and climate resilience programs in Nigeria to protect ecosystems, strengthen livelihoods, and support climate adaptation in vulnerable communities.

Program Overview

ACERDEN promotes environmental conservation and climate-resilient development to protect ecosystems, strengthen livelihoods, and support communities affected by climate change across Nigeria.

Environmental degradation and climate change pose serious threats to livelihoods, food security, water resources, and public health in Nigeria. Deforestation, soil erosion, flooding, desertification, and climate variability disproportionately affect rural and peri-urban communities.

ACERDEN’s Environment Conservation & Climate Resilience programs address these challenges through ecosystem protection, climate adaptation strategies, community education, and sustainable natural resource management. Our approach combines local knowledge, scientific research, and participatory action to build long-term environmental and climate resilience.

Key Focus Areas

  • Environmental Conservation & Restoration:Reforestation, afforestation, erosion control, and ecosystem rehabilitation.
  • Climate Change Adaptation & Resilience:Supporting communities to adapt to climate variability and extreme weather events.
  • Sustainable Natural Resource Management:Promoting responsible land, water, and forest use.
  • Community Environmental Education:Raising awareness on climate change, conservation, and sustainable practices.
  • Disaster Risk Reduction:Strengthening community preparedness for floods, droughts, and environmental shocks.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Participatory Environmental Assessments: Identifying local environmental risks and priorities with communities.
  • Nature-Based Solutions: Using environmentally sustainable interventions for conservation and restoration.
  • Capacity Building: Training community leaders, youth, and farmers in climate adaptation strategies.
  • Policy & Advocacy Engagement: Supporting evidence-based environmental planning and action.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation: Tracking environmental indicators and resilience outcomes over time.

Featured Project Highlights

  • Community Reforestation Initiative: Restoring degraded land and improving ecosystem services.
  • Flood & Erosion Control Projects: Reducing disaster risks in flood-prone communities.
  • Climate Awareness & Education Programs: Building knowledge and local action on climate change.

Why Environment & Climate Programs Work

  • Community Ownership: Local participation ensures sustainability and long-term stewardship.
  • Science-Informed: Combines indigenous knowledge with research-based approaches.
  • Integrated: Links environment, agriculture, health, and livelihoods.
  • Resilience-Focused: Prioritizes adaptation, preparedness, and risk reduction.

Key Achievements

250,000 Thousands of trees planted and degraded lands restored
500 Communities trained on climate adaptation and disaster preparedness
800 Reduced erosion and flooding risks in intervention areas
Rural Development

Rural Infrastructure Development

ACERDEN implements community-led development programs in Nigeria that strengthen local institutions, improve livelihoods, and promote sustainable social and economic growth.

Program Overview

ACERDEN empowers communities to identify their priorities, strengthen local systems, and drive sustainable development that improves quality of life for present and future generations.

Strong communities are the foundation of sustainable development. However, many rural and peri-urban communities in Nigeria face weak institutions, limited infrastructure, poor access to services, and low participation in decision-making processes.

ACERDEN’s Community Development programs strengthen grassroots institutions, promote inclusive participation, and support communities to plan, implement, and sustain development initiatives that reflect their real needs and priorities.

Key Focus Areas

  • Participatory Community Planning: Supporting communities to identify needs and design local solutions.
  • Institutional & Capacity Strengthening: Building skills of community-based organizations and local leaders.
  • Community Infrastructure Support: Facilitating small-scale infrastructure that improves daily living.
  • Social Cohesion & Inclusion: Encouraging participation of women, youth, and marginalized groups.
  • Livelihood & Economic Initiatives: Supporting community-driven income-generating activities.
  • Local Governance Engagement: Strengthening linkages between communities and local authorities.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Community Needs Assessments: Participatory rural appraisals and stakeholder consultations.
  • Grassroots Leadership Development: Training for community leaders and volunteers.
  • Inclusive Engagement: Ensuring women, youth, and vulnerable groups are represented.
  • Partnerships: Collaboration with local governments, CSOs, and development actors.
  • Monitoring & Learning: Tracking social, economic, and institutional outcomes.

Featured Project Highlightsh

  • Community Development Committees (CDCs): Strengthening grassroots governance structures.
  • Participatory Planning Initiatives: Communities co-design development priorities.
  • Livelihood Support Projects: Community-led income generation and skills initiatives.

Featured Project Highlightsh

  • Community-Owned: Solutions are driven and sustained by local people.
  • Inclusive: Prioritizes equity, participation, and social cohesion.
  • Scalable: Models can be adapted across regions.
  • Sustainable: Focuses on long-term institutional strength rather than short-term aid.

Key Achievements

Since November 2021, we've rehabilitated 85km of rural roads, constructed 12 bridges, and provided solar power to 15 community facilities. Transport costs have decreased by 40% in project areas, and access to markets has improved for over 5,000 rural households.

Health

Health & Social Development

ACERDEN delivers community-based health and social development programs in Nigeria, improving access to healthcare, disease prevention, and social protection for vulnerable populations.

Program Overview

ACERDEN works to improve health outcomes and social well-being by strengthening community healthcare access, preventing disease, and supporting vulnerable populations across Nigeria.

Health challenges remain a major barrier to development in Nigeria, particularly in rural and underserved communities. Limited access to healthcare services, preventable diseases, malnutrition, HIV/AIDS, and weak social protection systems continue to affect quality of life and productivity.

ACERDEN’s Health & Social Development programs adopt an integrated, community-driven approach to strengthen healthcare delivery, promote preventive health behaviors, and support socially vulnerable groups such as women, children, persons with disabilities, and orphans and vulnerable children (OVCs).

Key Focus Areas

  • Primary Healthcare Support: Strengthening access to basic healthcare services in underserved communities.
  • Maternal, Newborn & Child Health (MNCH): Improving outcomes for mothers and children through education and service access.
  • HIV/AIDS & Communicable Disease Interventions: Prevention, care, support, and stigma reduction.
  • Orphans & Vulnerable Children (OVC) Support: Health, education, and psychosocial assistance.
  • Health Education & Behavior Change Communication: Promoting healthy practices and disease prevention.
  • Social Protection & Inclusion: Supporting vulnerable groups to access essential services and social safety nets.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Community Health Outreach: Mobile clinics, health campaigns, and community health sessions.
  • Partnerships with Health Institutions: Collaboration with government and development partners.
  • Capacity Building: Training community health volunteers and caregivers.
  • Integrated Service Delivery: Linking health, nutrition, education, and social services.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation:Tracking health indicators and social outcomes for continuous improvement.

Featured Project Highlights

  • OVC Health & Education Support Program Integrated care for vulnerable children.
  • Community Health Outreach Initiatives:Bringing basic healthcare closer to rural households.
  • HIV/AIDS Prevention & Support Programs:Reducing transmission and supporting affected families.
  • Maternal & Child Health Campaigns:Improving health outcomes for mothers and children.

Why Our Health & Social Programs Are Effective

  • People-Centered: Designed around real community health needs.
  • Preventive Focus: Emphasizes education and early intervention.
  • Integrated Services: Links health, nutrition, and social protection.
  • Sustainable Impact: Builds local capacity for long-term outcomes.

Key Achievements

35% Expanded support for orphans and vulnerable children

Women & Youth Development

Women & Youth Development Programs in Nigeria | ACERDEN

ACERDEN empowers women and youth in Nigeria through skills development, leadership training, livelihood support, and social inclusion programs that promote sustainable development.

Program Overview

ACERDEN advances gender equality and youth empowerment by equipping women and young people with the skills, resources, and leadership opportunities needed to drive sustainable social and economic development.

Women and youth represent the majority of Nigeria’s population, yet they are disproportionately affected by poverty, unemployment, limited access to education, and social exclusion.

ACERDEN’s Women & Youth Development programs address these challenges by strengthening skills, promoting leadership, expanding livelihood opportunities, and fostering meaningful participation in community development and governance processes.

Key Focus Areas

  • Economic Empowerment: Skills training, entrepreneurship support, and income-generation initiatives.
  • Youth Employability & Skills Development: Vocational training, digital skills, and life-skills education.
  • Women’s Leadership & Participation: Supporting women’s inclusion in decision-making structures.
  • Gender Equality & Social Inclusion: Addressing barriers to participation and access to resources.
  • Youth Engagement & Civic Participation: Encouraging youth leadership in community and development initiatives.
  • Mentorship & Capacity Building: Coaching and mentorship for women and youth leaders.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Needs-Driven Design: Programs shaped by local realities and participant needs.
  • Community Partnerships: Working with women’s groups, youth associations, and local institutions.
  • Skills-Based Training: Practical, market-relevant skill development.
  • Integrated Support: Linking skills training with access to markets, finance, and networks.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation: Tracking economic, social, and leadership outcomes.

Featured Project Highlights

  • Women’s Economic Empowerment Initiatives: Supporting small-scale enterprises and cooperatives.
  • Youth Skills & Employability Programs: Vocational, digital, and life-skills training.
  • Leadership & Mentorship Programs: Building confidence and leadership capacity.

Why Women & Youth Development Works

  • Inclusive: Prioritizes marginalized and underserved groups.
  • Transformational: Builds confidence, leadership, and economic independence.
  • Sustainable: Focuses on long-term capacity rather than short-term support.
  • Community-Driven: Anchored in local structures and networks.

Key Achievements

Since September 2021, 1,200 women and youth have completed leadership training, and 850 have started income-generating activities. Women's participation in community decision-making bodies has increased from 15% to 45% in project communities, and youth unemployment has decreased by 30%.

Education

Education & Literacy Development in Nigeria

ACERDEN improves access to quality education and promotes literacy in Nigeria through community-based programs, school support, adult literacy, and inclusive learning initiatives.

Program Overview

Education and literacy are foundational to social and economic development. ACERDEN’s Education & Literacy Development programs focus on expanding access to quality education, improving learning outcomes, and addressing systemic barriers to literacy in underserved rural and peri-urban communities.

Our approach supports both formal and non-formal education, ensuring that children, youth, and adults especially women and vulnerable groups have opportunities to learn, grow, and participate meaningfully in society.

Key Focus Areas

  • Access to Basic Education: Supporting school enrolment, retention, and completion through community engagement and targeted educational support.
  • Adult & Functional Literacy: Providing adult literacy and numeracy programs that improve livelihoods, civic participation, and household well-being.
  • Girl-Child Education: Promoting girls’ education through advocacy, community sensitisation, and support initiatives that reduce gender-based barriers.
  • School Health & Learning Environment: Improving school infrastructure, hygiene facilities, and learning conditions to support student health and performance.
  • Teacher & Community Capacity Building:Strengthening the skills of teachers, school committees, and community education stakeholders.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Community Needs Assessment: Identifying education gaps with parents, teachers, and local leaders.
  • Participatory Program Design: Co-developing solutions with communities and education stakeholders.
  • Capacity Building: Training educators, facilitators, and community volunteers.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation: Tracking enrolment, attendance, literacy outcomes, and program impact.

Featured Program Highlights

  • Community-Based Adult Literacy Classes: Improving reading, numeracy, and life skills.
  • School Support Initiatives: Enhancing learning conditions and student engagement.
  • Girl-Child Education Advocacy Community-driven efforts to keep girls in school.

Why Education & Literacy Programs Work

  • Inclusive: Designed for children, youth, and adults.
  • Community-Owned: Strong involvement of parents and local leaders.
  • Integrated: Linked with health, WASH, gender, and livelihood initiatives.
  • Sustainable: Focused on capacity building and long-term learning outcomes.

Key Achievements

Since January 2019, we've supported 40 schools, trained 250 teachers, and provided scholarships to 500 vulnerable children. Student enrollment has increased by 35% in project schools, and standardized test scores have improved by an average of 42% across all supported institutions.

250 Improved school participation among children in underserved communities
500 Adult literacy programs supporting women and youth livelihood activities
42% Strengthened community involvement in school governance
Governance

Citizen Engagement & Accountability

ACERDEN strengthens democratic governance, civic participation, accountability, and civil society capacity in Nigeria through community-based and evidence-driven programs.

Program Overview

ACERDEN promotes inclusive governance, accountable institutions, and active citizen participation by strengthening civil society, empowering communities, and supporting democratic processes at the grassroots level.

Good governance and democratic participation are essential for sustainable development. ACERDEN’s Governance & Democracy programs focus on strengthening civic engagement, improving transparency and accountability, and empowering communities to actively participate in decision-making processes that affect their lives.

Our work supports grassroots democracy, strengthens civil society organisations, and promotes inclusive governance structures that respond to community needs—particularly in rural and underserved areas.

Key Focus Areas

  • Civic Education & Awareness: Enhancing citizens’ understanding of rights, responsibilities, and democratic processes.
  • Community Accountability & Transparency: Promoting social accountability mechanisms that strengthen service delivery and local governance.
  • Civil Society Strengthening: Building the institutional and technical capacity of civil society organisations and community groups.
  • Inclusive Participation: Supporting the participation of women, youth, and marginalized groups in governance and decision-making.
  • Policy Engagement & Advocacy: Facilitating dialogue between communities, civil society, and government institutions to influence policy and practice.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Participatory Needs Assessment: Identifying governance gaps with communities and stakeholders.
  • Capacity Building: Training civil society actors, community leaders, and volunteers.
  • Dialogue & Engagement: Facilitating town halls, forums, and multi-stakeholder discussions.
  • Monitoring & Learning: Tracking governance outcomes and strengthening accountability systems.

Why Governance & Democracy Programs Work

  • Community-Led: Designed with strong grassroots participation.
  • Rights-Based: Focused on inclusion, accountability, and equity.
  • Evidence-Driven: Informed by governance research and field data.
  • Sustainable: Builds long-term institutional and civic capacity.

Key Achievements

Since April 2022, we've established 25 community monitoring groups across 10 districts. Public participation in local budget hearings has increased from 10% to 65%, and citizen satisfaction with local services has improved from 35% to 75% based on our annual surveys.

25 Strengthened the capacity of local civil society organisations and networks
65% Increased community participation in local governance processes
75% Improved awareness of civic rights and responsibilities
Research

Research, Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) in Nigeria

ACERDEN delivers high-quality research, monitoring, and evaluation services in Nigeria to support evidence-based development, accountability, and learning across sectors.

Program Overview

ACERDEN strengthens development effectiveness by generating evidence, measuring impact, and supporting learning through robust research, monitoring, and evaluation systems.

Evidence-based decision-making is essential for sustainable development. ACERDEN’s Research, Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) program supports development actors, donors, and communities by delivering high-quality research, program monitoring, and impact evaluations across multiple sectors

We integrate rigorous methodology, community participation, and ethical standards to ensure credible findings that inform program design, policy development, and learning

Key Focus Areas

  • Baseline, Midline & Endline Studies: Designing and conducting studies to assess program needs, progress, and outcomes.
  • Program Monitoring Systems: Developing indicators, data collection tools, and reporting frameworks aligned with donor standards.
  • Impact Evaluation: Assessing program effectiveness, outcomes, and long-term sustainability.
  • Applied Development Research: Generating evidence on agriculture, health, WASH, environment, governance, and social development.
  • Learning & Knowledge Management: Supporting learning, adaptation, and dissemination of research findings.

Program Implementation Approach

  • Study Design & Methodology: Quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods approaches.
  • Ethical & Participatory Research: Community engagement and informed consent.
  • Data Collection & Analysis: Trained enumerators, digital tools, and robust analysis.
  • Reporting & Dissemination: Clear, actionable reports for diverse stakeholders.

Featured Research & Evaluation Highlights

  • Baseline Study for Rural Livelihood Programs: Informing targeted interventions.
  • Impact Evaluation of OVC Support Programs: Measuring social and education outcomes.
  • Monitoring Frameworks for Donor-Funded ProjectsEnsuring accountability and learning.

Featured Research & Evaluation Highlights

  • Technically Sound: Aligned with international M&E standards.
  • Context-Specific: Grounded in local realities and community engagement.
  • Independent & Credible: Objective, transparent, and ethical.
  • Action-Oriented: Focused on learning and program improvement.

Key Achievements

Since July 2020, we've completed 18 major research studies, published 25 policy briefs, and piloted 12 innovative development approaches. Our research findings have informed 5 national policy changes and improved the design of 8 major development programs implemented by partner organizations.

18 Conducted baseline and impact studies across multiple development sectors
12 Supported donor-funded projects with robust monitoring frameworks

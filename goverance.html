Governance & Democracy in Nigeria

ACERDEN promotes inclusive governance, accountable institutions, and active citizen participation by strengthening civil society, empowering communities, and supporting democratic processes at the grassroots level.

Support Democratic Governance

Program Overview

Good governance and democratic participation are essential for sustainable development. ACERDEN’s Governance & Democracy programs focus on strengthening civic engagement, improving transparency and accountability, and empowering communities to actively participate in decision-making processes that affect their lives.

Our work supports grassroots democracy, strengthens civil society organisations, and promotes inclusive governance structures that respond to community needs—particularly in rural and underserved areas.

Key Focus Areas

Civic Education & Awareness

Enhancing citizens’ understanding of rights, responsibilities, and democratic processes.

Community Accountability & Transparency

Promoting social accountability mechanisms that strengthen service delivery and local governance.

Civil Society Strengthening

Building the institutional and technical capacity of civil society organisations and community groups.

Inclusive Participation

Supporting the participation of women, youth, and marginalized groups in governance and decision-making.

Policy Engagement & Advocacy

Facilitating dialogue between communities, civil society, and government institutions to influence policy and practice.

Program Implementation Approach

Participatory Needs Assessment:

Identifying governance gaps with communities and stakeholders

Capacity Building:

Training civil society actors, community leaders, and volunteers

Dialogue & Engagement:

Facilitating town halls, forums, and multi-stakeholder discussions

Monitoring & Learning:

Tracking governance outcomes and strengthening accountability systems

Key Achievements

Strengthened the capacity of local civil society organisations and networks
Increased community participation in local governance processes
Improved awareness of civic rights and responsibilities
Enhanced collaboration between communities and public institutions

Frequently Asked Questions on ACERDEN Governance & Democracy Programs

What does ACERDEN mean by governance and democracy?

ACERDEN defines governance and democracy as inclusive decision-making, accountability, transparency, and active citizen participation at all levels.

Who participates in governance programs?

Community members, civil society organisations, women and youth groups, and local institutions participate in the programs.

Does ACERDEN work with government institutions?

Yes. ACERDEN collaborates with relevant government bodies while maintaining independence and community accountability.

Are women and youth included in governance initiatives?

Absolutely. Inclusion of women and youth is a core principle of ACERDEN’s governance work.

How does ACERDEN promote accountability?

Through social accountability tools, community monitoring, dialogue platforms, and evidence-based advocacy.

How is program impact measured?

Impact is measured through participation metrics, capacity assessments, governance outcome indicators, and qualitative feedback.

Can partners support governance programs?

Yes. Donors, NGOs, academic institutions, and development partners can support implementation, research, and capacity building.

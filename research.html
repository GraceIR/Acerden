Research, Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) in Nigeria

ACERDEN strengthens development effectiveness by generating evidence, measuring impact, and supporting learning through robust research, monitoring, and evaluation systems.

Partner With ACERDEN

Program Overview

Evidence-based decision-making is essential for sustainable development. ACERDEN’s Research, Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) program supports development actors, donors, and communities by delivering high-quality research, program monitoring, and impact evaluations across multiple sectors.

We integrate rigorous methodology, community participation, and ethical standards to ensure credible findings that inform program design, policy development, and learning.

Key Focus Areas

Baseline, Midline & Endline Studies

Designing and conducting studies to assess program needs, progress, and outcomes.

Program Monitoring Systems

Developing indicators, data collection tools, and reporting frameworks aligned with donor standards.

Impact Evaluation

Assessing program effectiveness, outcomes, and long-term sustainability.

Applied Development Research

Generating evidence on agriculture, health, WASH, environment, governance, and social development.

Learning & Knowledge Management

Supporting learning, adaptation, and dissemination of research findings.

Program Implementation Approach

Study Design & Methodology:

Quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods approaches

Ethical & Participatory Research:

Community engagement and informed consent

Data Collection & Analysis:

Trained enumerators, digital tools, and robust analysis

Reporting & Dissemination:

Clear, actionable reports for diverse stakeholders

Key Achievements

Conducted baseline and impact studies across multiple development sectors
Supported donor-funded projects with robust monitoring frameworks
Strengthened institutional M&E capacity for NGOs and community organisations
Produced research reports informing policy and program improvements

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What types of organizations does ACERDEN provide M&E services to?

ACERDEN works with NGOs, donor agencies, government institutions, academic researchers, and community-based organisations.

Does ACERDEN conduct independent evaluations?

Yes. ACERDEN provides independent, third-party monitoring and evaluation services.

What sectors does ACERDEN’s M&E cover?

M&E services span agriculture, health, WASH, environment, education, governance, and social development.

Does ACERDEN use digital data collection tools?

Yes. Digital tools are used to improve data accuracy, efficiency, and real-time monitoring.

How does ACERDEN ensure research ethics?

All studies follow ethical guidelines including informed consent, confidentiality, and community respect.

Can ACERDEN support proposal development with M&E frameworks?

Yes. ACERDEN supports project design with logical frameworks, indicators, and M&E plans.

Are research findings publicly available?

Many reports and learning materials are available through the Publications & Reports section, subject to partner agreements.

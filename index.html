Who Are We?

Welcome to ACERDEN

The African Centre for Rural Development & Environment (ACERDEN) is a Nigerian non-governmental, non-profit organization committed to advancing sustainable rural development, environmental protection, and poverty reduction. For over two decades, we have partnered with communities, civil society organisations, researchers, and development stakeholders to design and implement programmes that respond to real community needs. Our work integrates agriculture, health, environment, education, and social development to deliver long-term, measurable impact in underserved rural and peri-urban communities.

The African continent faces interlinked technological, human, social, economic, and environmental challenges that, if left unresolved, threaten basic life-support systems, weaken institutional structures, and perpetuate cycles of underdevelopment. Nigeria, like many developing countries, continues to grapple with food insecurity, widespread poverty, illiteracy, human suffering, and environmental degradation affecting both urban and rural populations. Access to quality education, healthcare, and safe drinking water remains grossly inadequate in many urban centres and is almost non-existent in numerous rural communities.

Our Purpose

Sustainable development refers to development that meets the needs of the present generation without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. Achieving this requires deliberate economic, social, and institutional transformation that empowers people, strengthens communities, and protects the environment.

Know About The CEO

Prof. Daniel Sunday Ugwu is a Professor/Reader in the Department of Agricultural Economics & Extension, Enugu State University of Science & Technology (ESUT). He is an Agricultural Economist by profession as well as a Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) Specialist.

Prof. Ugwu was the Ag. Programme Manager (1999 - 2000) and Director Planning, Monitoring & Evaluation (PME) (1991 - 2002) of a World Bank-Assisted Project, the Enugu State Agricultural Development Programme. He also served as the President of a Civil Society Organisation, the Enugu State Coalition of Civil Societies on HIV/AIDS (ESCCHA) (2003-2009).

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our programs and services

Yes. The African Centre for Rural Development & Environment (ACERDEN) is a registered Nigerian non-governmental, non-profit organization and has been operating since 1996.

ACERDEN works across rural development, agriculture and food security, environmental conservation and climate resilience, water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH), health and social development, women and youth empowerment, governance, and research, monitoring and evaluation.

ACERDEN implements programmes primarily in rural and peri-urban communities across multiple states in Nigeria, with a strong presence in South-East Nigeria.

Yes. ACERDEN collaborates with international and local donor agencies, NGOs, academic institutions, government bodies, and private sector partners on programme implementation, research, monitoring and evaluation, and capacity building.

ACERDEN combines over 25 years of field experience with strong community trust, multi-sector expertise, and robust research and monitoring & evaluation systems that ensure accountability, learning, and measurable impact.

ACERDEN applies evidence-based programme design, participatory monitoring, baseline and endline assessments, and transparent reporting aligned with donor and international development standards.

Organizations can partner with ACERDEN through project co-design, programme implementation, research collaboration, funding partnerships, and technical support. Interested partners can contact ACERDEN directly via the Partner With Us page.

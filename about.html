We Transform Lives

The African Centre for Rural Development & Environment (ACERDEN) is a non-profit, non-governmental, and non-political organization committed to sustainable rural development and environmental conservation in Nigeria since 1996.

ACERDEN was conceived out of the founder’s desire to improve the welfare of both urban and rural communities in Nigeria. The organization was registered with the Ministry of Health and Social Development (1996) and the Corporate Affairs Commission (1998).

Vision Statement

Partner With Us

Partnerships are central to achieving sustainable development at scale. ACERDEN works with donor agencies, NGOs, academic institutions, government bodies, and private sector partners to design and implement impactful, community-driven development programmes.

Learn More
Aims & Objectives
  • Improve living standards of Nigerians through sustainable development strategies.
  • Ensure food security and reduce poverty through agricultural and rural development programs.
  • Promote youth development, women empowerment, health, and education/literacy programs.
  • Encourage small-scale enterprises and indigenous technology for self-reliance.
  • Strengthen rural institutions, savings mobilization, and credit administration.
  • Promote environmental education and natural resources management.
  • Conduct research, advocacy, capacity-building workshops, and consultancy services.
Implementation Strategies
  • Public education and training programs.
  • Workshops, conferences, and seminars.
  • Advocacy & grassroots mobilization.
  • Research, feasibility, and impact studies.
  • Technology transfer and information dissemination.
  • Project planning, monitoring, and evaluation.
  • Networking with organizations sharing similar mandates.

Our Networks & Partnerships

ACERDEN actively collaborates with national and international organizations to maximize impact. Key networks include:

  • Christian Rural and Urban Development Association of Nigeria (CRUDAN)
  • National Network on Water and Sanitation in Nigeria (NEWSAN)
  • Association of NGOs for Orphans and Vulnerable Children (AONN)
  • Nigerian Network of NGOs for Population and Reproductive Health (NINPREH)
  • Civil Society Network on HIV/AIDS in Nigeria (CiSNHAN)
  • Enugu State Coalition of Civil Societies on HIV/AIDS (ESCCHA)
  • Civil Society Action Coalition on Education for All (CSACEFA)
  • Coalition of Eastern Non-governmental Organizations (CENGOS)

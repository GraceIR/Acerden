We Transform Lives

The African Centre for Rural Development & Environment (ACERDEN) is a non-profit, non-governmental, and non-political organization committed to sustainable rural development and environmental conservation in Nigeria since 1996.

ACERDEN was conceived out of the founder’s desire to improve the welfare of both urban and rural communities in Nigeria. The organization was registered with the Ministry of Health and Social Development (1996) and the Corporate Affairs Commission (1998).

Our Networks & Partnerships

ACERDEN actively collaborates with national and international organizations to maximize impact and drive sustainable change in rural communities.

CRUDAN

Christian Rural and Urban Development Association of Nigeria (CRUDAN)

Collaborating on faith-based development initiatives for sustainable community transformation across Nigeria.

NEWSAN

National Network on Water and Sanitation in Nigeria (NEWSAN)

Working together to improve water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) services in rural communities.

AONN

Association of NGOs for Orphans and Vulnerable Children (AONN)

Joint efforts in providing care, support, and protection for orphans and vulnerable children.

NINPREH

Nigerian Network of NGOs for Population and Reproductive Health (NINPREH)

Promoting reproductive health education and family planning services in rural areas.

CiSNHAN

Civil Society Network on HIV/AIDS in Nigeria (CiSNHAN)

Collaborating on HIV/AIDS prevention, treatment, and support programs.

ESCCHA

Enugu State Coalition of Civil Societies on HIV/AIDS (ESCCHA)

State-level collaboration for HIV/AIDS advocacy and community-based interventions.

CSACEFA

Civil Society Action Coalition on Education for All (CSACEFA)

Advocating for quality education and lifelong learning opportunities for all Nigerians.

CENGOS

Coalition of Eastern Non-governmental Organizations (CENGOS)

Regional network for capacity building, advocacy, and collective action among NGOs in Eastern Nigeria.

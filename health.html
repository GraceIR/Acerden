Health & Social Development

ACERDEN works to improve health outcomes and social well-being by strengthening community healthcare access, preventing disease, and supporting vulnerable populations across Nigeria.

Support our work

Program Overview

Health challenges remain a major barrier to development in Nigeria, particularly in rural and underserved communities. Limited access to healthcare services, preventable diseases, malnutrition, HIV/AIDS, and weak social protection systems continue to affect quality of life and productivity.

ACERDEN’s Health & Social Development programs adopt an integrated, community-driven approach to strengthen healthcare delivery, promote preventive health behaviors, and support socially vulnerable groups such as women, children, persons with disabilities, and orphans and vulnerable children (OVCs).

Key Focus Areas

Primary Healthcare Support:

Strengthening access to basic healthcare services in underserved communities.

Maternal, Newborn & Child Health (MNCH):

Improving outcomes for mothers and children through education and service access.

HIV/AIDS & Communicable Disease Interventions:

Prevention, care, support, and stigma reduction.

Orphans & Vulnerable Children (OVC) Support:

Health, education, and psychosocial assistance.

Health Education & Behavior Change Communication:

Promoting healthy practices and disease prevention.

Social Protection & Inclusion:

Supporting vulnerable groups to access essential services and social safety nets.

Program Implementation Approach

Community Health Outreach:

Mobile clinics, health campaigns, and community health sessions.

Partnerships with Health Institutions:

Collaboration with government and development partners.

Capacity Building:

Training community health volunteers and caregivers.

Integrated Service Delivery:

Linking health, nutrition, education, and social services.

Monitoring & Evaluation:

Tracking health indicators and social outcomes for continuous improvement.

Key Achievements

Improved access to essential health services in rural communities
Expanded support for orphans and vulnerable children
Increased community awareness on disease prevention and healthy living
Strengthened linkages between communities and healthcare providers

Frequently Asked Questions on ACERDEN Health and Social Development

What health services does ACERDEN provide?

ACERDEN supports primary healthcare, maternal and child health, disease prevention, and social health services.

Does ACERDEN work on HIV/AIDS programs?

Yes. ACERDEN implements prevention, care, and support programs, including community education.

Who benefits from ACERDEN’s health programs?

Rural communities, women, children, OVCs, and other vulnerable populations

How does ACERDEN support orphans and vulnerable children?

Through healthcare access, education support, psychosocial care, and family strengthening.

Does ACERDEN partner with government health agencies?

Yes. ACERDEN collaborates with public and private health institutions.

Are community members involved in program delivery?

Yes. Community health volunteers and leaders play active roles.

How is health impact measured?

Using health indicators, service utilization data, and outcome assessments
